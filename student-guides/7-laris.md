# Laris Case Study: Student Guide

## Purpose
This guide will help you prepare for a productive discussion of the Laris case study. The goal is to develop your thinking about hiring bar calibration, stakeholder alignment, organizational decision-making patterns, and the use of "roving engineer" models.

## How to Use This Guide

1. **Read the case study thoroughly** (7-laris.md)

4. **Come prepared** to discuss your perspectives and listen to others

## Case Study Overview

**Key Players:**
- **Laris**: New engineering manager for Starfleet project (referred by Jack)
- **Agnes**: Roving lead engineer with extensive company knowledge, recently moved to Starfleet
- **Jack**: Senior engineer on Starfleet, referred Laris, on hiring committee
- **Elnor**: Senior engineer on Starfleet, worked with Agnes before, on hiring committee
- **Starfleet Team**: Small team of less senior engineers working on critical project
- **Leadership**: Desperate to see Starfleet shipped, keeps moving Agnes to crisis projects

**The Situation:**
Agnes proposed a new modular approach and wants to hire a lead engineer for her sub-team. After 8 weeks and 5 candidates, no hire has been made despite rigorous screening. The hiring committee (including Elnor and Jack) hasn't given unanimous approval to any candidate. Starfleet is critical but stuck.

## Pre-Discussion Reflection Questions

### Understanding the Context

1. **What is Agnes's role and history at Acme?**
   - "Roving lead engineer" who moves between teams
   - Helped many teams with difficult challenges
   - Worked with both Elnor and Jack before
   - What does this pattern tell us?

2. **What is Starfleet's importance?**
   - "Could easily establish an almost insurmountable lead against competition"
   - "Growth starting to flatten out in other areas"
   - "Few other substantial ideas"
   - Company is "desperate to see Starfleet shipped"
   - What pressure does this create?

3. **What happened when Agnes joined Starfleet?**
   - Took 2 weeks to "gain her bearings"
   - Recommended more modular approach
   - Asked to create new sub-team
   - Leadership "eagerly agreed" and created hiring budget
   - What does this tell us about Agnes's influence?

### Understanding the Hiring Challenge

4. **What's happening in the hiring process?**
   - 8 weeks, 5 candidates, no hire
   - Agnes does "very rigorous" screening
   - Committee includes Agnes, Elnor, Jack as senior members
   - No "unanimous approval" for any candidate
   - What might "very rigorous" mean?

5. **Why might there be no unanimous approval?**
   - Different standards or expectations
   - Different assessment of candidates
   - Different priorities for the role
   - Interpersonal dynamics
   - Strategic disagreements
   - What else?

6. **What are the costs of not hiring?**
   - Time lost (8 weeks already)
   - Starfleet still not progressing
   - Opportunity cost
   - Candidate experience
   - Team morale

### Understanding the Stakeholders

7. **Put yourself in Agnes's shoes:**
   - Brought in to fix crisis (again)
   - Sees a solution (modular approach)
   - Needs team to execute it
   - Can't hire anyone
   - What might Agnes be feeling?

8. **Put yourself in Elnor's shoes:**
   - Founding member of Starfleet
   - Worked with Agnes before on scaling
   - On hiring committee
   - What concerns might Elnor have?

9. **Put yourself in Jack's shoes:**
   - Founding member of Starfleet
   - Worked with Agnes before on econometrics
   - Referred Laris for manager role
   - On hiring committee
   - What might Jack be thinking?

10. **Put yourself in Laris's shoes:**
   - New to the team and company
   - Referred by Jack (has relationship)
   - Team is stuck, can't hire, project critical
   - Needs to "figure out what might be going on"
   - What challenges does Laris face?

## Diagnostic Questions

**What should Laris investigate?**

**To understand the hiring process:**
- What does "unanimous approval" mean? Why is it required?
- What feedback has been given on each candidate?
- Where do evaluators disagree and why?
- What is the hiring bar and is it calibrated?

**To understand the technical strategy:**
- What is the modular approach Agnes proposed?
- Do Elnor and Jack agree with it?
- How was this decision made?
- What alternatives were considered?

**To understand team dynamics:**
- How does the existing team feel about Agnes's arrival?
- What is the relationship between Agnes, Elnor, and Jack?
- How do decisions typically get made?
- Who has what authority?

**To understand the pattern:**
- How many times has Agnes been moved to teams?
- What happens after Agnes fixes things?
- How do teams feel about this pattern?
- What incentives does this create?
